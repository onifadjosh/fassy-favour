Olubi Construction

Olubi Construction is a premier corporate platform designed to showcase the scale, precision, and technical expertise of a leading civil engineering firm. The website functions as a digital portfolio and business development tool, featuring high-resolution project galleries and structured service categories that establish brand authority. By blending architectural aesthetics with a user-centric information architecture, the platform simplifies the journey for potential clients from exploring massive infrastructure projects to initiating professional consultations and service inquiries

Live Link

Problem Statement

The construction industry often struggles with a digital presence that fails to reflect the physical quality of their work. Olubi Construction needed to move away from fragmented offline portfolios and a static web presence that didn't communicate their full range of services or technical safety standards. Potential clients lacked a centralized place to view high-resolution project galleries, verify certifications, or easily request quotes, leading to missed opportunities in a highly competitive bidding market.

Solution

The solution was to design a robust, visually-driven corporate platform that highlights Olubi Construction’s track record through an interactive project showcase. I focused on creating a clean, professional UI that uses architectural photography and bold typography to convey "strength" and "reliability." The site features a categorized project portfolio, a detailed services section, and a strategic lead-generation form designed to capture specific project requirements from potential clients, effectively turning the website into a business development tool.

Overview

Thesis Archive is a web-based platform designed to streamline how academic thesis papers are submitted, accessed, and reviewed. It was built to solve the common issue of disorganized, hard-to-access final-year projects in many institutions. The platform allows students to upload their thesis directly, while lecturers can review submissions in one centralized space. By digitizing the process, Thesis Archive helps preserve academic work, encourages accountability, and supports a more efficient supervision system.

Role

Product Designer & Frontend Developer

Tools

UX Process

  1. Research & Discovery: Conducted informal interviews with students and lecturers to understand pain points in thesis submission and access.
    • Identified key challenges: manual documentation, lack of access to past work, and unclear supervision flow.
  2. User Flow Mapping: Created simple flows for students (upload/view thesis) and lecturers (review access).
    • Focused on making the platform minimal and distraction-free.
  3. Wireframing: Sketched out low-fidelity wireframes to plan structure and layout.
    • Created a consistent design system to maintain visual harmony across all screens.
  4. High-Fidelity Design: Designed a clean UI using Figma with clear sections for all key actions.
    • Prioritized typography and spacing to ensure readability and simplicity.
  5. Prototype & Feedback: Built interactive prototypes and shared with test users.
    • Gathered insights and made final adjustments.
