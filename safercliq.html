SaferCliq AI

SaferCliq AI is a conversational hospitality platform that simplifies guest communication through a custom-designed WhatsApp AI interface. It empowers hotel guests to lodge complaints and requests instantly, ensuring seamless service delivery and high-level guest satisfaction through an intuitive, human-centered design.

Live Link

Problem Statement

In the traditional hospitality industry, guest communication is frequently hindered by significant friction and operational delays. Guests often face intimidating communication barriers, such as relying on physical front desks or in-room telephones, which feel outdated in a digital-first world. This disconnection leads to slow response times and a fragmented user journey, where requests are easily lost or disorganized due to a lack of centralized, real-time tracking systems. Without a unified platform to capture and resolve concerns instantly, hotels struggle with operational bottlenecks and unresolved issues, ultimately resulting in guest frustration and a decline in overall service quality.

Solution

The solution was to develop SaferCliq, an AI-powered hospitality assistant integrated directly into WhatsApp to meet guests where they are most comfortable. By leveraging conversational AI, the platform provides a seamless, "stress-free" channel for guests to lodge complaints and request services instantly, eliminating the need for physical contact or phone calls. The system automates the routing of these requests to the appropriate hotel departments, ensuring real-time tracking and faster resolution times. This digital transformation not only streamlines hotel operations and reduces human error but also creates a more responsive, human-centered experience that preserves service quality and enhances overall guest satisfaction.

Overview

SaferCliq is an AI-powered hospitality assistant designed to bridge the gap between hotel guests and management through a familiar, conversational interface. Developed as a WhatsApp-integrated solution, the platform empowers guests to lodge complaints, request amenities, and seek assistance instantly without the friction of traditional front-desk interactions. By leveraging human-centered design principles and intelligent automation, SaferCliq transforms the guest experience into a seamless, stress-free journey while providing hotel staff with a centralized system to track and resolve service requests in real-time. This project highlights the intersection of UX storytelling and technical implementation, creating a digital ecosystem where efficiency meets high-touch hospitality.

Role

Product Designer

UX Process

  1. Research & Ecosystem Mapping: Conducted interviews with hospitality staff to map the full journey from a guest lodging a complaint to a staff member resolving the ticket.
    • Identified the need for a two-way sync: a simple mobile UI for guests and a high-utility data dashboard for management.
  2. Conversational & Logic Design: Mapped out the AI's dialogue flows to ensure the WhatsApp interaction felt human and responsive.
    • Focused on minimizing the number of messages a guest needs to send to get their issue logged.
  3. Admin Dashboard Architecture: Designed a robust backend interface focused on data visualization and task management.
    • Included real-time status badges (Pending, Ongoing, Resolved) and analytics cards to track overall hotel service health.
  4. High-Fidelity Prototyping: Created pixel-perfect designs in Figma for both the mobile WhatsApp screens and the desktop admin portal.
    • Used a consistent design system to ensure the branding felt unified across different platforms and screen sizes.
  5. Testing & Iteration: Tested the prototype with a focus on "Handover" logic—ensuring the transition from AI automation to human staff intervention was seamless.
    • Refined the dashboard's notification system based on feedback to prevent "alert fatigue" for staff members.
